I am 35 years old, male, and currently live in Germany. I have been through a lot, with many highs and lows. I want to pass on the wisdom I have gathered from it, in the hope that it helps you gain a new perspective on life.

## The Logo

![](/logo.png)

The flower in the logo grows out of a crack to catch the light of the sun.
In the same way, we grow through the adversities of life, seeking out cracks and crevices. We do not grow despite the difficulties but because of them.
The result is a beautiful blossom that was shaped by the difficulties in the first place, and chooses to bloom.

This belief in development and in the freedom we all have as human beings is what the logo is meant to symbolize.
Through which resistances have you grown?

## AI

All texts are written entirely by me. Wherever there are translations, it is noted that they were generated automatically. Sometimes I start an article in English, sometimes in German.

AI is used for the development of this very simple blog. The main focus of this blog should be the text, not the code underneath. Hence the minimalism.

The technology I use is Next, Nextra and Tailwindcss as well as mdx (markdown).
